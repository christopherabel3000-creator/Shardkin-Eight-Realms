# Shardkin: Eight Realms

A turn-based monster-collecting RPG, built to be fully playable with a screen reader (VoiceOver on iPhone, or any screen reader on a computer).

**Play it:** https://christopherabel3000-creator.github.io/shardkin-eight-realms/

## Current state
A World 1 (Plant) prototype:

- **Camp menu:** Levels, Team and base, Shop, Synthesis, Buildings, Training, Achievements, Saved game.
- **Five levels** on 36-space dice boards with wild battles, healing springs, thorn traps, portals and quiet paths, a look-ahead of the next six spaces, and Loaded Dice to pick your roll.
- **Level bosses** that must be defeated, followed by a **reward wheel**.
- **Battles** with element strengths, free switching, capturing, potions and team-wide experience.
- **All 15 Plant monsters**, with evolutions at level 16 and final forms unlocked by crests.
- **Player levels** that unlock the Shop, Training Yard and Shard Mine.
- **Synthesis** scrolls for Rare, Epic and Legendary Plant monsters.
- **Daily training** in time-based or experience-based mode, and **passive income** from the Shard Mine.
- **Achievements** with rewards.
- **Automatic saving** in your browser, and optional sound effects.

## Accessibility
Every screen section has a heading (for example What happened, Level board, Look ahead, Opponent, Skills, Switch monster), and all actions are buttons. After each action, focus moves to "What happened" so the results of the turn are read aloud right away.

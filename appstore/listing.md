## Description (4,000)

Shardkin: Eight Realms is a turn-based monster-collecting RPG you can play entirely by ear. Long ago the Heartcrystal shattered, and each shard grew a realm of its own. You are a Shardbinder: build a team, travel shard by shard, and put the Heartcrystal back together.

COLLECT 120 SHARDKIN
Fifteen monsters for each of eight elements: Plant, Water, Fire, Earth, Electric, Psychic, Ghost and Physical. Catch them in the wild, hatch them from eggs, or combine them with Scrolls of Synthesis. Rare monsters evolve, and the rarest reach a final form with a crest of their own. Every species has its own trait, description and cry.

ROLL THROUGH BOARD-GAME LEVELS
Each level is a board of 36 spaces. Roll the die and land on wild battles, healing springs, thorn traps, portals, treasure chests and a wandering merchant, with a boss waiting at the end. Look ahead to see what's coming, or use a Loaded Die to choose your roll.

BATTLE WITH REAL CHOICES
Element strengths, sleep, stun and poison, free switching, support skills and charms. Capture what you weaken, or win and share the experience with your whole team.

A STORY TO THE END
Twelve worlds and 170 levels. Your rival Sable waits at the end of every world, and a final battle waits in the Phantom Rift. After it come the Trials, an endless Arena, roaming world bosses and weekly rules.

BUILD YOUR CAMP
A shop, a Training Yard, a Shard Mine, expeditions, a hatchery, the Shard Forge, daily and weekly quests and achievements. Auto-play replays the levels you've already cleared.

MADE FOR VOICEOVER
Shardkin was designed from the start to be fully playable with VoiceOver. Every screen has headings, every action is a button, and every result is read clearly. Sounds carry information too: each element has its own attack sounds, each monster its own cry, and optional health tones rise and fall with a monster's health.

PLAY WITH A FRIEND
Trade a monster or battle a friend's team with codes you can send in a message. Live duels put each of you on your own device.

YOURS TO KEEP
The whole game works offline and saves as you play. An optional cloud save or save code moves your game to another device.

No ads. Nothing to buy. No accounts. No tracking.

## App Privacy

- Does the app collect data? Yes, because of optional cloud saves and live duels.
- Data type: User Content, then Gameplay Content. (This covers the saved game, teams, and the Shardbinder name inside them.)
- Used for: App Functionality only.
- Linked to the user's identity: No. Saves are stored under a random key and duels under a random code, not an account.

## Notes for the reviewer

Apple asks every new app for six things in this field: a screen recording made on a real iPhone, and answers 2 to 6 below. Only the recording link changes.

Shardkin: Eight Realms is a complete monster-collecting RPG, built to be fully playable with VoiceOver by a developer who plays with a screen reader. The build was tested on a physical iPhone through TestFlight before submission.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, starting from launching the app: https://youtu.be/CMtRL0N7Brg
The voice heard in the recording is VoiceOver, since the developer plays the game with a screen reader.
The app has no account registration or login, no paid content or purchases, and no user-generated content beyond a player's chosen name, which is shown only to the one friend they share a duel code with. So those flows don't exist to show.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
A turn-based monster-collecting role-playing game. The player builds a team of up to four monsters, rolls a die through board-game levels, battles and captures 120 original monsters, and restores the Heartcrystal across twelve worlds. It is for fans of monster-collecting and turn-based strategy games, and especially for blind and low-vision players: very few games of this kind work with a screen reader, and this one was designed for VoiceOver from the start, with every screen, action and result exposed as text and standard controls, and sounds that carry information.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No account, login or sample files are needed. From the main menu tap Levels, then World 1, then Level 1. Tap Roll the die to move along the board, and choose a skill when a battle starts. Everything else is reachable from the main menu.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Only one, and it is optional: Google Firebase Realtime Database, used under Friends on the main menu for Cloud save (move a game to another device, stored under a random key) and live duels between two friends who share a four-letter code. There are no authentication services, payment processors, ad networks, analytics or AI services. The whole game is bundled in the app and runs offline; saves are kept on the device, including as a file in the app's Documents folder.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region. It is in English only. There is no region-specific content or behavior.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRIES AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry. It contains no third-party or licensed material: every monster, name, world and story element is original. The two fonts are used under the SIL Open Font License. There are no purchases; both in-game currencies are earned only by playing.

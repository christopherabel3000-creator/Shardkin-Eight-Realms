## Description (4,000)

Shardkin: Eight Realms is a turn-based monster-collecting RPG you can play entirely by ear. Long ago the Heartcrystal shattered, and each shard grew a realm of its own. You are a Shardbinder: build a team, travel shard by shard, and put the Heartcrystal back together.

COLLECT 120 SHARDKIN
Fifteen monsters for each of eight elements: Plant, Water, Fire, Earth, Electric, Psychic, Ghost and Physical. Catch them in the wild, hatch them from eggs, or combine them with Scrolls of Synthesis. Rare monsters evolve, and the rarest reach a final form with a crest of their own. Every species has its own trait, description and cry.

ROLL THROUGH BOARD-GAME LEVELS
Each level is a board of 36 spaces. Roll the die and land on wild battles, healing springs, thorn traps, portals, treasure chests and a wandering merchant, with a boss waiting at the end. Look ahead to see what's coming, or use a Loaded Die to choose your roll.

BATTLE WITH REAL CHOICES
Element strengths, sleep, stun and poison, free switching, support skills and charms. Capture what you weaken, or win and share the experience with your whole team.

A STORY TO THE END
Twelve worlds and 170 levels. Your rival Sable waits at the end of every world, and a final battle waits in the Phantom Rift. After it come the Trials, an endless Arena, roaming world bosses and weekly rules.

BUILD YOUR CAMP
A shop, a Training Yard, a Shard Mine, expeditions, a hatchery, the Shard Forge, daily and weekly quests and achievements. Auto-play replays the levels you've already cleared.

MADE FOR VOICEOVER
Shardkin was designed from the start to be fully playable with VoiceOver. Every screen has headings, every action is a button, and every result is read clearly. Sounds carry information too: each element has its own attack sounds, each monster its own cry, and optional health tones rise and fall with a monster's health.

PLAY WITH A FRIEND
Trade a monster or battle a friend's team with codes you can send in a message. Live duels put each of you on your own device.

YOURS TO KEEP
The whole game works offline and saves as you play. An optional cloud save or save code moves your game to another device.

No ads. Nothing to buy. No accounts. No tracking.

## App Privacy

- Data type: User Content, then Gameplay Content. (This covers the saved game, teams, and the Shardbinder name inside them.)

- Linked to the user's identity: No. Saves are stored under a random key and duels under a random code, not an account.

- Nothing else is collected: no analytics, no ads, no identifiers. The contact form opens in Safari, outside the app.

## Notes for the reviewer

Shardkin: Eight Realms is a complete monster-collecting RPG, built to be fully playable with VoiceOver by a developer who plays with a screen reader. The whole game is bundled in the app and runs offline. Saves are kept on the device (including as a file in the app's Documents folder). There are no purchases; both in-game currencies are earned only by playing.

To try it: from the main menu tap Levels, then World 1, then Level 1. Tap Roll the die to move along the board, and choose a skill when a battle starts. No account or sign-in is needed.

The only network use is optional and found under Friends on the main menu: Cloud save, and live duels between two friends who share a four-letter code. Both use Google Firebase. Rival codes and trade codes on the same screen are plain text and work offline. A player's chosen name is shown only to the friend they share a code with.

## Encryption

The app only uses standard HTTPS, so it's exempt. `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is already set to NO in Info.plist, so App Store Connect won't ask.

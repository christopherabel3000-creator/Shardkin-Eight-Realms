Privacy policy

Shardkin: Eight Realms, by playbyear. Last updated November 16, 2026.

Shardkin: Eight Realms is a monster-collecting game you mostly play on your own. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains the little information the game handles and where it goes.

What stays on your device

Your game is saved on your phone or computer: in the browser's storage on the website, and in the app's own storage and Documents folder in the iPhone app. That includes everything in your game, such as your Shardbinder name and the nicknames you give your monsters, plus settings like whether sound is on. None of it is sent anywhere unless you use a cloud save or a live duel. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, deletes it.

Cloud saves, if you use them

Cloud saves are optional. When you choose Save to the cloud, the game sends a copy of your saved game to an online database run with Google's Firebase service. The copy is stored with:

  • your 12-character cloud key, which the game makes up at random,
  • the time of the save, and
  • a random code for the device that saved it, made up by the game and kept on that device, so the game can tell when a save was changed on another device.

Nothing in a cloud save identifies you, unless you type something that does, such as your real name as your Shardbinder name. Anyone who has your cloud key can load or replace that save, so keep the key private, like a password. Nobody can list or browse the saves; a save can only be found with its key.

Cloud saves are kept so you can load them later. You can delete yours at any time in the game: open Friends, go to Cloud save, and choose Delete the cloud save. Your saved game and the device code are removed. All that's left is a marker that the key was deleted, and when, so the key can't load anything again. The game on your device isn't affected.

Live duels, if you play them

Live duels are optional. When you host or join a duel, the game stores the duel in the same online database so both players' devices can follow it. A duel holds:

  • its four-letter duel code,
  • both players' Shardbinder names and copies of the teams they brought,
  • the moves chosen and what happened in the battle, and
  • the time of the last move and the random device code of whoever made it.

The player you share the duel code with sees your Shardbinder name and your team. Nobody can list or browse duels; a duel can only be found with its code. A duel isn't removed the moment it ends. Once nobody has touched it for a week it can be cleared away, which happens when its code comes up again for a new duel.

Google's part

When a cloud save or duel is sent or loaded, Google receives your device's internet address as part of the connection, as any website does. Google handles it under its own terms as a service provider; the game doesn't keep or use it. You can read Firebase's privacy information.

Save codes, rival codes and trade codes

These codes are parts of your game written out as text: your whole game, your team, or one monster. The game only makes one when you ask, and it stays wherever you put it, such as your notes or a message to a friend. It isn't sent anywhere by the game.

Children

The game isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. It never asks for any.

Changes

If the game ever handles information differently, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions, problems, or anything about your privacy: use the contact form, and say that it's about Shardkin. You don't need an account, and only the game's developer sees what you send. Add your email address only if you'd like a reply; it's used just to answer you. Messages are kept in Google Forms, under Google's terms.

How to play this prototype
  • Camp has a menu: Levels, Team and base, Shop, Synthesis, Buildings, Training and Achievements. New areas unlock as your player level rises.
  • Pick up to four monsters for your team, then choose a level. Your team is locked once the level starts.
  • On the board, roll the die to move along 36 spaces: wild battles, healing springs, thorn traps, portals and quiet paths. Look ahead lists the next six. A Loaded Die from the shop lets you pick your roll.
  • Healing springs and thorn traps affect your lead monster. Rolling past the end lands you on the boss space. Bosses must be defeated, not captured, and then you spin the reward wheel.
  • Fainted monsters stay out for the rest of the level. If your whole team faints, the level fails, but you keep your captures and experience.
  • Switching is free in battle. Skills, items and capture attempts use your turn. Capture chance is 1 minus current health divided by max health, times a rarity factor.
  • Every defeat or capture gives experience to each standing monster on your team. Rare, Epic and Legendary monsters evolve at level 16. Epics and Legendaries reach a final form with their own crest item.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser on this device.

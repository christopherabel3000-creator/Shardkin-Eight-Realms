How to play this prototype
  • Camp has a menu: Levels, Team and base, Shop, Synthesis, Buildings, Training and Achievements. New areas unlock as your player level rises.
  • Pick up to four monsters for your team, then choose a level. Your team is locked once the level starts. Clearing a world's last level unlocks the next world. After the eight element worlds come combination worlds that mix two elements.
  • On the board, roll the die to move along 36 spaces: wild battles, healing springs, thorn traps, portals, treasure chests, a wandering merchant and quiet paths. Look ahead lists the next six. A Loaded Die lets you pick your roll.
  • Rolling past the end lands you on the boss space. Level bosses must be defeated, not captured, and then you spin the reward wheel.
  • Fainted monsters stay out for the rest of the level. If your whole team faints, the level fails, but you keep your captures and experience.
  • Some skills can put a monster to sleep, stun it or poison it. Sleeping or stunned monsters lose their turns, and poison deals damage each round. Status effects raise capture odds.
  • Rarely, a world boss hides on an ordinary-looking space and moves every roll. When you find it, fight now (ending that level attempt) or ignore it and fight later from camp. Its damage carries over between fights, you earn one fight every 2 hours (up to 5), and it leaves after 24 hours.
  • Every defeat or capture gives experience to each standing monster on your team. Rare, Epic and Legendary monsters evolve at level 16. Epics and Legendaries reach a final form with their own crest.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser on this device.

How to play this prototype
  • At camp, pick up to four monsters for your team, then choose a level. Your team is locked once the level starts.
  • On the board, roll the die to move along 36 spaces. Spaces can hold wild battles, healing springs, thorn traps, portals or a quiet path. Look ahead lists the next six.
  • Healing springs and thorn traps affect your lead monster, the one that starts each fight. Battle, spring and trap spaces are cleared once used.
  • Rolling past the end lands you on the boss space. The level boss must be defeated, not captured. Then you spin the reward wheel.
  • Fainted monsters stay out for the rest of the level. If your whole team faints, the level fails, but you keep your captures and experience.
  • Switching is free in battle. Skills, items and capture attempts use your turn. A failed capture gives the wild monster a free action.
  • Capture chance for a Common is 1 minus its current health divided by its maximum. Uncommons are three quarters of that, Rares half.
  • Every defeat or capture gives experience to every standing monster on your team, even ones that didn't fight. Fainted monsters miss out.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser on this device.

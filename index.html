    How this prototype works
    • Each battle is against a random team of two wild monsters from the Plant world, weighted toward common ones.
    • Switching is free: you switch and can still act. Skills, items and capture attempts use your turn.
    • Capture odds rise as the target's health drops and fall with rarity. A failed throw gives the wild monster a free action.
    • Strong hits deal 1.5 times damage, weak hits about two thirds. A skill matching its user's element gets a small bonus.
    • "Next battle" keeps your team as it is, like moving on through a level, so fainted monsters stay out. "Restore team" resets everything.
    • Your team includes two test monsters so you can try switching. A real new player starts with Cindercub alone.
    • Skills 3 and 4 unlock at levels 8 and 15 for now. All numbers are first guesses for playtesting.

Auto-play

How to play this prototype
  • Camp opens on the main menu, grouped into Adventure, Monsters and Camp. Choose an area to open just that area, then use Back to main menu to return. Each menu button notes anything waiting for you, like quest rewards or eggs ready to hatch. New areas unlock as your Shardbinder level rises.
  • Pick up to four monsters for your team, then choose a level. Your team is locked once the level starts. Clearing a world's last level unlocks the next world. After the eight element worlds come combination worlds that mix two elements.
  • On the board, roll the die to move along 36 spaces: wild battles, healing springs, thorn traps, portals, treasure chests, a wandering merchant and quiet paths. Look ahead lists the next six. A Loaded Die lets you pick your roll.
  • Rolling past the end lands you on the boss space. Level bosses must be defeated, not captured, and then you spin the reward wheel.
  • Fainted monsters stay out for the rest of the level. If your whole team faints, the level fails, but you keep your captures and experience.
  • Some skills can put a monster to sleep, stun it or poison it. Sleeping or stunned monsters lose their turns, and poison deals damage each round. Status effects raise capture odds.
  • Rarely, a world boss hides on an ordinary-looking space and moves every roll. When you find it, fight now (ending that level attempt) or ignore it and fight later from camp. Its damage carries over between fights, you earn one fight every 2 hours (up to 5), and it leaves after 24 hours.
  • Every defeat or capture gives experience to each standing monster on your team. Rare, Epic and Legendary monsters evolve at level 16. Epics and Legendaries reach a final form with their own crest.
  • Cleared levels can be auto-played. The die rolls itself and skills are picked for you, choosing the strongest matchup. Settings let you change the speed, repeat the level, use Potions and catch monsters you don't have yet.
  • About 1 in 500 wild monsters is Shardborn, a crystal variant with 10% stronger stats. A chime marks one when it appears. Shard storms on the board make them ten times more likely for the rest of the level.
  • Wild monsters, bosses, chests, storms, expeditions, the Arena and the Shard Mine drop elemental shards. Spend them at the Shard Forge on items and eggs, or to infuse monsters of the same element.
  • Rewards shrink once a monster has outgrown a level: full rewards up to 3 levels above its monsters, then less for each extra level. New worlds unlock better items, like Super Potions, Revive Shards and Greater Binding Shards.
  • Shard Dust is the everyday currency from battles, chests and the Shard Mine. Coins are rare and come only from milestones like first-time world clears, achievements, weekly quests and world bosses. Spend Coins in the Coin Shop on Mythic Eggs, Shardborn Lures, Chosen Crests and more.
  • Progress saves automatically in this browser on this device. Use Copy save code and Load a save code in Settings to back up your game or move it to another device.

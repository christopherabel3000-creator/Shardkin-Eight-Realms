Support

Shardkin: Eight Realms, by playbyear.

Shardkin: Eight Realms is a turn-based monster-collecting game. Build a team of up to four monsters, roll the die through board-game levels, battle and capture 120 Shardkin, and restore the Heartcrystal across twelve worlds. It works fully with VoiceOver and other screen readers.

Get help or send feedback

Use the contact form for bug reports, questions and ideas, and say that it's about Shardkin. You don't need an account. Add your email address if you'd like a reply. For a bug, it helps to say what you were doing, which screen you were on, and whether you use VoiceOver.

Common questions

Is my game saved?

Yes. The game saves on its own as you play, on your device. In the iPhone app it's also kept as a file in the app's own storage, which iOS doesn't clear. Deleting the app deletes the save, so make a cloud save or copy a save code first.

How do I move my game to another device?

Open Friends and go to Cloud save. Choose Save to the cloud to get a 12-character key, then enter that key on the other device. You can also use Copy save code in Settings, which gives you your whole game as text, and load it on the other device. The website and the app can load each other's saves.

I lost my cloud key.

The game shows it under Cloud save, in Friends, on the device that made it. Nobody else can look it up, not even the developer, because saves can't be listed or searched.

Does it need the internet?

No. The whole game works offline, including rival codes and trade codes. Only live duels and cloud saves need a connection.

How do I play with a friend?

Open Friends. A rival code lets a friend battle a copy of your team in their own game, whenever they like. A trade code sends one of your monsters to a friend. A live duel is a battle with each of you on your own device: one of you hosts and reads out the four-letter code, and the other joins with it.

Does it have ads, purchases or tracking?

No ads, nothing to buy, no accounts and no tracking. Shard Dust and Coins are earned by playing and can't be bought. See the privacy policy.

How do I hear what a monster sounds like, or turn sounds down?

Every Shardkin has its own cry, played from the Collection. Settings has sound effects, monster cries, health tones and Brief messages, which shortens what's read in battle.
